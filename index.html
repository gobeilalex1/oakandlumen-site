    Questions

    Good to know

    Anything else? Write to us at support@oakandlumen.com.

    What is the minimum order?

    It depends on the product. We quote the lowest quantity the factory will run, and the price per piece drops as the quantity goes up.

    How long does an order take?

    It depends on the product and how it ships. Your quote gives you the delivery window before you pay anything.

    Where are the products made?

    At factories we pick for each product. Every item is labeled with its country of origin.

    How do I pay?

    By bank transfer to Arc Commerce LLC. A deposit starts production, and the balance is due when the order is ready to ship.

    Can I see it first?

    Every custom order starts with a proof you approve, and nothing is made until you do.

    Why are there no prices in the catalog?

    Your price depends on the quantity, the logo method and freight to your door. Add what you like and we send one delivered price, so there is no math left for you to do.

    What if I do not see what I want?

    Tell us. Most of what we make is not listed here. If a factory can make it with your logo, we can quote it.

    Is the quoted price final?

    Yes, until the date on your quote. It includes freight and import duties to your door. If you change the order, we re-quote before you pay anything.
