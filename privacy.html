Who we are

Oak & Lumen is operated by Arc Commerce LLC, Arizona, United States.

What we collect

If you ask for a quote or email us, we receive what you send: your name, business, email address and the details of your request. This site does not use tracking cookies or advertising pixels. Its fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

How we use it

Only to reply to you, prepare your quote and handle your order. Factories and carriers receive what they need to make and deliver an order. Quote requests sent through the form on this site are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit, a form delivery service. We do not sell your information.

Business emails

We sometimes email businesses at addresses they publish on their own websites. Reply "no" to any of those emails and we will not email you again.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email support@oakandlumen.com.
